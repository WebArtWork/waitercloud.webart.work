Waiter Cloud

Food is more than a restaurant name

Waiter Cloud connects the people, recipes, places, schools, jobs and stories behind the food industry.

Discover what you want to eat, who created it, where they work now, how recipes evolve, where to learn, and where new opportunities are waiting.

01

Follow the people behind the food

You loved a dish at a restaurant, but a few months later it tastes completely different. Maybe the chef who created it is no longer there.

Waiter Cloud connects recipes with their authors and workplaces, so you can see who created the food you love, follow their career and discover where they are cooking now.

02

Search for the dish, not only the restaurant

Sometimes you don't care which restaurant you visit. You just want a great Carbonara.

Search for a recipe or dish and discover nearby restaurants that serve it, the people behind it, related recipes and community feedback.

03

Recipes have a history

A great recipe rarely stays exactly the same. People improve it, adapt it and create their own versions.

Public recipes can be forked into completely new recipes while keeping a connection to the original, letting you discover how an idea evolved and who created each version.

04

Build a real food-industry profile

A CV tells you where somebody worked.

Waiter Cloud can show what they actually created.

Chefs, waiters and other food-industry professionals can build public profiles connected to recipes, workplaces, experience and feedback — giving restaurants a better picture of the person behind an application.

05

Find work — or get discovered

Employees can search and apply for jobs, but opportunities don't have to start with an application. Restaurants can discover professionals and send proposals directly.

Applications and proposals can then become contracts, keeping the whole employment journey connected in one place.

06

Discover where to learn

Choosing a cooking school should involve more than reading a description. Explore cooking schools, their location, community feedback and the people connected with them.

Over time, Waiter Cloud can help you see what students learned, what they created and where their careers took them.

07

Save food for later

Finding something interesting is only the beginning. Save recipes you want to try, keep track of what you're planning to cook and mark what you've already made.

Your Workspace becomes your personal food collection rather than another endless feed you forget tomorrow.

08

Understand places through people and food

Restaurant ratings alone don't tell the whole story. Waiter Cloud connects restaurants with recipes, chefs, employees and feedback, giving you more context about why people love a place — and what may have changed when the experience changes.
